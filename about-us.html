About Us

Who We Are

ScalarVerse is a leading online esports tournament management platform operating in India. We are dedicated to providing a professional, transparent, and secure environment for organizing and participating in skill-based gaming competitions. Our mission is to democratize esports by enabling players, teams, and gaming enthusiasts to create, organize, and participate in tournaments with ease and confidence.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and accessible platform for esports tournament management in India, fostering a community of skilled gamers and promoting fair, transparent, and merit-based competition in digital sports.

What We Do

ScalarVerse provides a comprehensive suite of tools and services for organizing and managing esports tournaments. Our platform streamlines the entire tournament lifecycle, from creation and registration to bracket management, match scheduling, live result tracking, and prize distribution.

Services Offered

  • Tournament Creation and Management: Easy-to-use tools for tournament organizers to create tournaments with customizable formats, rules, and schedules.
  • Participant Registration: Seamless registration process for players and teams to join tournaments of their choice.
  • Tournament Brackets: Automated bracket generation supporting single-elimination, double-elimination, round-robin, and Swiss-system formats.
  • Match Scheduling: Intelligent scheduling system that optimizes match timings and venue management.
  • Live Results and Leaderboards: Real-time result tracking and dynamic leaderboard updates for transparency and engagement.
  • Prize Management: Secure and transparent prize pool management with automated distribution to winners.
  • Skill-Based Gaming Events: Hosting and managing competitive gaming events across multiple supported titles.
  • Digital Event Hosting: Complete platform support for online tournament hosting with integrated communication tools.

Nature of Our Platform

ScalarVerse is an esports tournament management and digital event hosting platform. All competitions conducted on our platform are:

  • Skill-Based: Outcomes are determined entirely by player skill, strategy, and in-game performance.
  • Fair and Transparent: All tournaments follow standardized rules with fair play enforcement.
  • Non-Gambling: We do not engage in gambling, betting, wagering, or chance-based activities.
  • Entertainment-Focused: Our platform is classified as entertainment and sports event hosting, not gaming in the regulatory sense.

Participation Model

Players can participate in tournaments on our platform by:

  • Creating an account and completing verification
  • Browsing available tournaments
  • Paying participation fees (where applicable) to join tournaments of their choice
  • Competing in matches based on predefined tournament rules
  • Receiving prizes based on their performance and tournament outcomes

Entry Fees and Prize Pools

To ensure fair competition and maintain prize pools, some tournaments on our platform may require entry fees from participants. These fees are:

  • Used to fund prize pools and cover platform operational costs
  • Processed securely through authorized payment gateways
  • Governed by our Refund & Cancellation Policy
