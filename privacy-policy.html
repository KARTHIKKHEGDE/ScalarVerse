Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 22, 2026

ScalarVerse Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us", "our", or "ScalarVerse") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your personal information when you use our website, mobile application, and services (collectively, the "Platform").

Important Notice: This Privacy Policy is compliant with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, as applicable in India.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information Provided by You

When you use the Platform, we collect the following personal information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, residential address, and user ID.
  • Identity Verification Information: Government-issued identification numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID), photograph, and verification details.
  • Payment Information: Bank account details, UPI ID, credit/debit card information (processed through secure payment gateways, not stored directly on our servers), and transaction history.
  • Tournament Information: Tournament registration details, participant profile, gaming username/ID, gaming skill level, and tournament participation history.
  • Communication Information: Messages, support tickets, feedback, and communications with our team.
  • KYC Documents: Know Your Customer (KYC) documents and verification records.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain information when you access and use the Platform:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, unique device identifiers, IP address, and device settings.
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, time spent on pages, links clicked, features used, and interaction with the Platform.
  • Location Information: General location data based on IP address (state/city level, not precise).
  • Log Information: Server logs including access times, pages requested, and error information.

1.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activities and preferences. These include:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: Cookies that remain on your device for a specified period.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used to understand how users interact with the Platform.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to track advertising effectiveness and personalize marketing messages.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

We collect and process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: Creating and maintaining your account, authentication, and identity verification.
  • Service Delivery: Providing tournament management services, processing registrations, scheduling matches, and managing results.
  • Payment Processing: Processing entry fees, prize distributions, and financial transactions securely.
  • Compliance and Legal Obligations: Complying with Indian laws including KYC requirements, tax regulations, and RBI guidelines.
  • Fraud Prevention: Detecting, preventing, and investigating fraudulent activities, cheating, and unauthorized access.
  • Customer Support: Addressing your inquiries, resolving disputes, and providing technical support.
  • Platform Improvement: Analyzing usage patterns, improving features, and optimizing user experience.
  • Marketing and Communications: Sending promotional offers, tournament updates, newsletters, and platform announcements (with your consent).
  • Fair Play Enforcement: Monitoring tournament integrity, enforcing rules, and maintaining ethical standards.
  • Dispute Resolution: Handling complaints, grievances, and resolving conflicts.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

3.1 Data Storage

  • Personal data is stored on secure servers located within India, in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000.
  • We maintain multiple backups of critical data to prevent loss.
  • Sensitive data such as payment information is encrypted using industry-standard protocols.
  • Data is stored for the duration of your account and for a reasonable period after account closure for legal and compliance purposes.

3.2 Data Security Measures

  • Encryption: Sensitive data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS protocols and at rest using AES-256 encryption.
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel with a legitimate need have access to personal data.
  • Firewalls and Intrusion Detection: Our systems are protected by firewalls and continuous monitoring.
  • Regular Audits: We conduct regular security audits and penetration testing.
  • Password Protection: Your account is protected by a secure password, which only you know.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication: We offer optional two-factor authentication for enhanced security.
  • Data Minimization: We collect and retain only the minimum personal data necessary for service delivery.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Third Parties with Whom We Share Data

We may share your personal information with the following third parties under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Payment Gateways: Paytm, Razorpay, Google Pay, PhonePe, and other authorized payment processors for transaction processing.
  • Banks and Financial Institutions: For processing payments and refunds.
  • Government Agencies: Where required by law or for regulatory compliance.
  • Law Enforcement: When required by legal process or to prevent imminent harm.
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting providers, email services, SMS providers, and analytics tools.
  • Tournament Organizers: Your tournament participation details and performance records (only with consent).

4.2 Data Sharing Restrictions

  • We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties for commercial purposes.
  • We do NOT share sensitive personal data (SPI) such as payment information without explicit consent and secure protocols.
  • Third-party service providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and data protection obligations.
  • Any sharing of data complies with Indian privacy laws and regulations.

5. Use of Cookies

5.1 Cookie Types and Purposes

We use cookies for various purposes including session management, user preferences, security, analytics, and marketing. You can control cookie settings in your browser. However, disabling cookies may limit your ability to use certain features of the Platform.

5.2 Cookie Management

  • You can delete cookies from your browser settings.
  • You can set your browser to reject cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent.
  • Third-party cookies from analytics and advertising partners may be used with your consent.
  • We respect "Do Not Track" signals and will not set tracking cookies if you enable this setting.

6. User Rights Regarding Personal Data

6.1 Your Rights Under Indian Law

You have the following rights regarding your personal data in accordance with Indian privacy laws:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request and receive a copy of your personal data held by us.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Right to Restriction: You can request that we limit the use of your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw consent for data processing at any time.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You can file complaints with data protection authorities.
  • Right to Object: You can object to marketing communications and certain types of processing.

6.2 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@scalarverse.com with clear identification and specific details of your request. We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.

7. Data Retention

  • Active account data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • Transaction records are retained for 7 years in compliance with tax regulations.
  • KYC documents are retained for a minimum of 5 years as per RBI guidelines.
  • Dispute-related data is retained for a period of 5 years from the date of resolution.
  • Marketing data is retained until you unsubscribe from communications.
  • Log files and analytics data are retained for 90 days unless required for legal purposes.
  • Upon account deletion, we will delete personal data within 60 days, except where retention is required by law.
