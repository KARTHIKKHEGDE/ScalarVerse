Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: January 22, 2026

This Refund and Cancellation Policy ("Policy") governs all refund requests, entry fee cancellations, and prize distributions on the ScalarVerse Platform. Please read this policy carefully as it outlines the conditions under which refunds are allowed and when refunds are not permissible.

Important Notice: ScalarVerse operates as a professional esports tournament management platform. Entry fees are participation fees for skill-based competitions. All refund policies comply with payment gateway regulations and Indian law. Please note that once you join a tournament and the tournament begins, entry fees are generally non-refundable.

1. Refund Policy Overview

1.1 Refund Eligibility

Refunds are provided under specific circumstances as detailed in this policy. To be eligible for a refund, the request must be made in writing to refunds@scalarverse.com with supporting documentation.

1.2 Processing Authority

All refund requests are reviewed and processed by the ScalarVerse Refunds and Dispute Resolution Team. Decisions regarding refund eligibility are final and binding.

2. When Refunds Are Allowed

2.1 Tournament Cancellation by Organizer

  • If a tournament is cancelled by the organizer before the tournament starts, full refund of entry fees will be processed.
  • Cancellation notice and refund will be issued within 48 hours of cancellation decision.
  • Refunds will be processed to the original payment method used for entry fee payment.

2.2 Platform-Initiated Cancellation

  • If the Platform cancels a tournament due to technical issues, force majeure, or regulatory requirements, full refund will be issued.
  • Force majeure events include natural disasters, internet outages, payment gateway failures, or government actions.
  • Refund will be processed within 5-7 business days.

2.3 Pre-Tournament Cancellation by User

  • If you request to withdraw from a tournament before the tournament officially starts, a partial or full refund may be considered.
  • For withdrawal requests made more than 48 hours before tournament start time: 90% refund will be provided (10% administrative fee deducted).
  • For withdrawal requests made 24-48 hours before tournament start time: 75% refund will be provided (25% administrative fee deducted).
  • For withdrawal requests made less than 24 hours before tournament start time: No refund will be provided.
  • Withdrawal requests must be submitted in writing to refunds@scalarverse.com.

2.4 Duplicate Payment or Payment Error

  • If you have been charged twice for the same tournament entry, full refund of the duplicate charge will be processed.
  • If a payment error occurred (e.g., incorrect amount charged), full refund of the erroneous amount will be issued.
  • Refund requests for payment errors must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction.

2.5 Technical Issues or Platform Errors

  • If your entry was not properly recorded due to Platform technical errors, full refund will be processed.
  • If you were unable to participate due to Platform downtime on your part (not due to your internet/device), refund may be considered.
  • Technical issue refunds require documentation and investigation.
  • Refund decision will be communicated within 15 days of submission.

2.6 Fraudulent Transaction

  • If your payment method was used fraudulently without your authorization, full refund will be processed.
  • Fraudulent transaction claims must be reported immediately with supporting evidence.
  • We will cooperate with payment gateways to investigate and resolve fraud cases.

3. When Refunds Are NOT Allowed

3.1 Tournament Already Started

  • Once a tournament has officially started (first match is live), entry fees are non-refundable even if you have not played.
  • This policy applies to all participants regardless of their performance or participation status.
  • If you registered but did not participate in the tournament, no refund is provided.

3.2 User Disqualification for Rule Violations

  • If you are disqualified for cheating, hacking, match-fixing, collusion, or violating tournament rules, entry fee is non-refundable.
  • Any winnings earned before disqualification will be forfeited.
  • Entry fees paid by disqualified users are retained by the Platform.

3.3 Change of Mind

  • Refunds are not provided simply because you changed your mind about participating.
  • Buyer's remorse is not grounds for refund under this policy.
  • This applies even if the tournament has not started yet.

3.4 Poor Tournament Performance

  • Refunds are not provided because you performed poorly or did not win prizes.
  • Your skill level, ranking, or match outcomes are not valid refund reasons.
  • All tournaments are skill-based, and participation is voluntary.

3.5 Personal Circumstances

  • Refunds are not provided for personal reasons such as illness, emergency, or inability to participate.
  • Changes in personal plans or schedule are not grounds for refund.
  • Financial difficulties do not qualify for refund consideration.

3.6 Account-Related Issues

  • If your account was suspended or terminated due to violation of Terms and Conditions, refunds are not provided.
  • Refunds are not provided if you forgot your password or lost access to your account.
  • Entry fees remain forfeited if your account status changes due to your actions.

5. Prize Distribution Policy

5.1 Standard Prize Distribution

  • Prizes are distributed to tournament winners based on their final ranking.
  • Prize distribution follows the structure specified in tournament details.
  • All prizes are processed through the same payment gateway used for entry fee collection.
  • Prize processing typically takes 7-14 business days from tournament conclusion.

5.2 Prize Distribution Delays

  • If prizes are delayed due to Platform issues, interest at the rate of 8% per annum will be credited (up to 60 days of delay).
  • If prizes are delayed due to payment gateway issues, the Platform will follow up with the payment provider.
  • Delayed prize claims must be reported within 30 days of expected distribution.

5.3 Prize Forfeiture

  • If a winner is disqualified for rule violations after tournament completion, their prize is forfeited.
  • Forfeited prizes are redistributed to the next-ranked eligible participants or retained by the Platform.
  • If a winner's account is terminated, prizes are not distributed.

6. Refund Processing

6.1 How to Request a Refund

  • Submit refund requests in writing to refunds@scalarverse.com.
  • Include your account details, tournament name, transaction reference, and reason for refund request.
  • Provide supporting documentation (screenshots, payment receipts, error messages, etc.).
  • Refund requests must be submitted within 60 days of the transaction.

6.2 Refund Processing Timeline

  • Refund requests are reviewed within 15 business days of submission.
  • Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval.
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method used for entry fee payment.
  • In case of payment gateway delays, the Platform will follow up with the provider.

6.3 Refund Mode

  • Refunds are processed to the same payment method used for the original transaction.
  • If the original payment method is not available, refund will be processed via alternative method after verification.
  • Bank transfers may take 3-5 business days to reflect in your account.
  • Wallet refunds (in UPI/Paytm) typically appear within 1-2 business days.

7. Dispute Resolution

7.1 Dispute Process

  • If you disagree with a refund decision, you can escalate to the Grievance Officer.
  • Escalation requests must be submitted within 7 days of receiving the refund decision.
  • The Grievance Officer will review and respond within 15 days.
  • Final appeal can be made to the Chief Compliance Officer within 7 days of the Grievance Officer's decision.

7.2 Chargeback and Payment Gateway Disputes

  • If you file a chargeback with your bank/card issuer without first requesting a refund from the Platform, your account may be suspended.
  • We will cooperate with payment gateways and your bank to resolve disputes.
  • False chargebacks may result in account termination and blacklisting.

8. Finality Clause

All refund decisions made by ScalarVerse are final and binding. Once a refund decision has been made and communicated, no further appeals or disputes regarding that specific refund will be entertained. This policy and all refund decisions are subject to the Terms and Conditions and applicable Indian law. The jurisdiction for all disputes is exclusive to courts in Bangalore, Karnataka.

9. Special Circumstances

9.1 User-Initiated Withdrawals

  • Users can withdraw from a tournament before it starts (see Section 2.3).
  • Once the tournament starts, you cannot withdraw and no refund will be provided.
  • Withdrawal requests are irrevocable once submitted.

9.2 Account Closure

  • If you close your account, any remaining balance or pending prizes will be refunded or distributed as per policy.
  • Closure requests must be submitted in writing to account-closure@scalarverse.com.
  • Processing typically takes 15-30 days.
